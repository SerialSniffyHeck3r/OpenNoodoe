# OpenNoodoe privacy policy
Developer: Veritas Studio
Updated: September 16, 2026
Contact: shin6361@gmail.com

OpenNoodoe connects directly to a compatible dashboard over Bluetooth. It has no account system, Internet permission, advertising or analytics SDK. It does not automatically send data to the developer.

On-device data
The app processes and stores selected photos, imported themes, preferences, Bluetooth device details, dashboard/system information, the last observed odometer value and diagnostic records. Records can include device identifiers, Bluetooth addresses, Android/phone details, commands and dashboard responses.

Permissions and dashboard transfers
Bluetooth permissions enable discovery and communication. On Android 11 and earlier, location permission is used for Bluetooth discovery; the app does not track location. Notifications show the connection-service status. Files are selected through Android. Photos, themes and setting/time commands are sent to the paired dashboard when you use these features.

Storage and deletion
Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local app data. Original files elsewhere on the phone, any copies you made and content transferred to the dashboard must be managed separately. Android system backup is enabled; backup and device transfer depend on the device and OS settings. The developer does not operate this backup service.

Support and external services
If you email the developer, the information you voluntarily provide is used to respond and investigate your request. Redact personal details from logs before sending them. You can request deletion of information you sent by contacting the email above. Email, Google Play and any separately provided beta signup form operate under their own applicable privacy notices.

Changes
This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to shin6361@gmail.com.

OpenNoodoe is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by KYMCO or Noodoe.
